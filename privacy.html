Privacy Policy for Job Wizard

Last updated: 13 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Job Wizard ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

Who We Are

Job Wizard is developed by Alwyn Kotze, an independent developer based in South Africa. If you have questions about this policy, you can contact us at alwynbkotze@gmail.com.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password (handled securely via Firebase Authentication). We do not store your password in plain text — this is managed entirely by Google Firebase's authentication service.

Customer and Job Data

As you use the App to manage your business, you may enter:

  • Customer names and contact information (phone numbers, email addresses, addresses)
  • Job details, notes, and pipeline status
  • Follow-up bookings and scheduling information

This data is stored securely in Google Firestore and is only accessible to your account.

Voice Recordings

The App allows you to record voice notes attached to jobs, and via the Easy Notes feature. These recordings are transcribed to text, and the original audio is deleted after transcription. Only the resulting text is retained and is accessible only to your account. We do not use voice recordings for any purpose beyond the feature you record them for.

Photos

You may attach photos to jobs (e.g. before/after photos, documentation). These are stored securely and are only accessible to your account.

Financial Data

The App's financial dashboard processes data you enter about job pricing, invoicing, and earnings to display summaries and reports. This financial data is stored in your account and is not shared with third parties, except as required to process subscription payments (see "Payments" below).

Contacts (Optional Feature)

If you use the "Add Customer from Contacts" feature, the App requests access to your device's contacts (READ_CONTACTS permission) so you can select an existing contact to quickly add as a customer. We only access contact information you explicitly select — the App does not read, upload, or store your entire contacts list. Only the specific contact details you choose to import (name, phone, email) are saved as customer data within the App.

Payments

Subscriptions (R119/month with a 30-day free trial) are processed through Google Play Billing. We do not directly collect or store your payment card details — this is handled entirely by Google Play.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Provide and operate the core functionality of the App (job pipeline management, customer tracking, financial reporting)
  • Maintain your account and authenticate your login
  • Process subscription payments via Google Play Billing
  • Improve and troubleshoot the App

We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising purposes.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Google Firebase (Firestore for structured data, Firebase Storage for photos, Firebase Authentication for login credentials). Firebase applies industry-standard security practices, including encryption in transit. Access to your data within the App is restricted to your authenticated account.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data will be deleted within 30 days, except where we are required to retain certain records for legal or accounting purposes.

Your Rights

You may:

  • Access, update, or delete your customer and job data directly within the App
  • Request a copy of your data by contacting alwynbkotze@gmail.com
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting alwynbkotze@gmail.com

Children's Privacy

Job Wizard is intended for use by tradespeople and business owners and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
